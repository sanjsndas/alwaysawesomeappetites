Cookies Policy

Understand how Yard Sauce uses cookies to enhance your experience, and exercise control over your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, enhance user experience, and provide valuable analytical data to site owners. Cookies do not typically contain personally identifiable information, but they can store preferences and help us understand how you interact with our digital properties.

At Yard Sauce, we use cookies to deliver a seamless browsing experience, remember your preferences, and improve our services. This Cookies Policy explains what types of cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

Our commitment: We believe in transparency. This policy gives you full visibility into our cookie practices, so you can make informed decisions about your privacy.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies based on their function and duration. Below is a detailed breakdown:

a. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core features such as page navigation, secure login, and session management. Without these cookies, our website would not operate correctly, and you would not be able to use basic services like requesting estimates or accessing your account.

  • Session cookies: Temporarily store your session ID to keep you logged in during your visit.
  • Security cookies: Help protect against cross-site request forgery and other malicious activities.

b. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect aggregated data about how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most frequently, how long users spend on each page, and if they encounter errors. This information helps us optimize content and improve site performance. All data is anonymized and cannot be traced back to you personally.

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to track user behavior and generate reports on site traffic.
  • Heatmap tools: We may use session recording tools to understand user interactions and improve usability.

c. Functional & Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences, such as your language preference, region, or the items you have added to your quote basket. They enhance your experience by personalizing content and saving you from having to re-enter your preferences each visit.

  • Language selection: Stores your preferred language for future visits.
  • Form data: Remembers partially filled forms to prevent data loss.

d. Marketing & Targeting Cookies

With your explicit consent, we may use cookies to deliver targeted advertisements relevant to your interests. These cookies track your browsing activity across different websites and may be placed by third-party advertising networks. We use these cookies only if you have opted in via our consent banner.

  • Retargeting: Shows you relevant Yard Sauce ads on other platforms after you have visited our site.
  • Social media: Integrates with platforms like Facebook and Instagram for sharing and advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that we integrate into our website. These third parties may set their own cookies to provide functionality, analytics, or advertising. We do not control these cookies, and they are subject to the third party's own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies for more information.

  • Google Analytics: Provides anonymized usage data. Learn more
  • Facebook Pixel: Helps us measure ad performance and build audiences. Learn more
  • YouTube: If we embed videos, YouTube may set cookies. Learn more

5. How We Use Cookie Data

We use the data collected via cookies for the following purposes:

  • Website improvement: Analyzing traffic patterns to identify popular content and optimize navigation.
  • Personalization: Customizing content and offers based on your preferences and browsing history.
  • Marketing effectiveness: Measuring the success of our advertising campaigns and refining our messaging.
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraudulent or malicious activity.
  • Compliance: Meeting legal and regulatory obligations regarding data protection.

6. Your Consent and Control

We respect your right to choose how cookies are used on your device. When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our website.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, but please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Here are links to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

7. Cookie Duration: Session vs. Persistent

Cookies can be either session-based or persistent:

  • Session cookies: These cookies are temporary and are deleted from your device when you close your browser. They are used to maintain your session and enable core functionality.
  • Persistent cookies: These cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you manually delete them. They are used to remember your preferences and settings for future visits.

8. Updates to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our business practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Revised" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. If we make significant changes, we will notify you via a prominent notice on our website.

9. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals your preference to websites. At present, we do not respond to DNT signals because there is no universally accepted standard for handling them. However, you can control cookie usage through your browser settings and our consent banner as described above.
