Our Story

Bringing Sunshine to Your Kitchen

Founded in 2010, Always Awesome Appetites began as a small food blog dedicated to sharing recipes that bring joy and warmth to the table. Our founder, Chef Amelia Sunshine, believed that food should not only nourish the body but also uplift the spirit.

With over 1,200 original recipes in our collection, we've grown into a trusted resource for home cooks seeking delicious, reliable recipes with a touch of sunshine. Each recipe is tested multiple times in our test kitchen to ensure perfect results every time.

Our yellow theme represents the warmth, energy, and happiness that good food brings to our lives. From bright citrus desserts to golden baked goods, we celebrate the vibrant colors and flavors that make cooking an enjoyable adventure.
