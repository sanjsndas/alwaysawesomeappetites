Our Story

Discover the passion, people, and philosophy behind Always Awesome Appetites

Bringing Sunshine to Kitchens Worldwide

Founded in 2010, Always Awesome Appetites began as a small food blog dedicated to sharing recipes that bring joy and warmth to the table. Our founder, Chef Amelia Sunshine, believed that food should not only nourish the body but also uplift the spirit.

What started as a passion project has blossomed into a thriving community of home cooks, food enthusiasts, and culinary adventurers. With over 1,200 original recipes in our collection, we've grown into a trusted resource for those seeking delicious, reliable recipes with a touch of sunshine.

Our signature yellow theme represents the warmth, energy, and happiness that good food brings to our lives. From bright citrus desserts to golden baked goods, we celebrate the vibrant colors and flavors that make cooking an enjoyable adventure.

Our Mission

To empower home cooks with tested, accessible recipes that bring joy to the kitchen and warmth to the table.

"We believe that cooking should be an enjoyable, creative experience—not a stressful chore. Every recipe we share is designed to be approachable, reliable, and most importantly, delicious. We're here to help you create meals that will make your loved ones smile."

— Chef Amelia Sunshine, Founder

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Passion for Food

We genuinely love cooking and believe that passion is the most important ingredient in any recipe.

Tested & Trusted

Every recipe is tested multiple times in our kitchen to ensure perfect results for you at home.

Community Focused

We listen to our readers and create content that addresses their needs, questions, and cravings.

Sustainability

We promote seasonal cooking, reduce food waste, and suggest sustainable ingredient choices.

Education

We don't just share recipes—we teach techniques, explain why methods work, and build cooking confidence.

Joyful Approach

Cooking should be fun! We emphasize enjoyment over perfection in the kitchen.
