Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I submit my own recipe to be featured on your site?

We love featuring recipes from our community! Please send your recipe along with high-quality photos to submissions@awesomeappetites.com. Our team reviews all submissions and will contact you if we decide to feature your recipe. Please allow 2-3 weeks for a response.

Can I use your recipes in my cookbook or on my website?

All recipes on Always Awesome Appetites are copyrighted. If you'd like to use one of our recipes, please contact us at permissions@awesomeappetites.com for licensing information. We're usually happy to grant permission for non-commercial use with proper attribution.

Do you offer cooking classes or workshops?

Yes! We host monthly cooking classes at our Golden Valley test kitchen. Classes range from beginner basics to advanced techniques. Check our website's "Events" section for the current schedule and booking information. We also offer virtual classes for those who can't join us in person.

I have dietary restrictions. Can you help modify a recipe?

Absolutely! Many of our recipes include substitution suggestions for common dietary needs (gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, etc.). If you need help adapting a specific recipe, email our recipe hotline at recipes@awesomeappetites.com and one of our chefs will provide personalized suggestions within 48 hours.
