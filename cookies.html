Cookies Policy

Learn how we use cookies to enhance your experience on Always Awesome Appetites

Last Updated: November 15, 2023

Welcome to Always Awesome Appetites! We believe in transparency, so we want you to understand how and why we use cookies on our website. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree to our use of cookies, you can adjust your browser settings to disable them or choose to not use our site.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you use our website
  • Personalize your experience
  • Improve our content and services
  • Show you relevant advertisements

Types of Cookies We Use

We use several types of cookies on Always Awesome Appetites:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website.

  • Session management
  • Security features
  • Load balancing
  • Cannot be disabled

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences to personalize your experience on our website.

  • Language preferences
  • Recipe favorites
  • Display settings
  • Shopping lists

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Page visit counts
  • Time spent on pages
  • Traffic sources
  • Popular recipes

Marketing Cookies

These cookies track your browsing habits to show you advertisements that are more relevant to you and your interests.

  • Ad personalization
  • Retargeting campaigns
  • Conversion tracking
  • Social media integration

Third-Party Cookies

We also use cookies from trusted third parties. These may include:

  • Google Analytics: To understand how our website is used and how we can improve it.
  • Social Media Platforms: Facebook, Pinterest, and Instagram cookies to enable sharing and integration features.
  • Advertising Partners: To show you relevant ads and measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.
  • Payment Processors: If you purchase our cookbook or merchandise, payment processors may set cookies for transaction security.

Please note that we have no control over third-party cookies. You should check the respective privacy policies of these third parties for more information about their cookies and how to manage them.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to choose whether to accept cookies. Here are the ways you can control cookies:

Browser Controls: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser. Common browser controls include:

  • Deleting all or specific cookies
  • Blocking third-party cookies
  • Setting notifications when cookies are set
  • Incognito/Private browsing modes

What Happens If You Disable Cookies?

If you choose to disable cookies, please be aware that some features of our website may not function properly:

  • You may need to re-enter your preferences each time you visit
  • Your saved recipes and shopping lists may not be retained
  • Some personalized content may not be available
  • Certain features may not work as intended

Essential cookies are required for the website to function, so these cannot be disabled. If you disable these, you may not be able to use certain parts of our website.

Updates to This Policy

We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information on our cookie practices. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
